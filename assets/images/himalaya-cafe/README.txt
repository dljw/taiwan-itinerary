himalaya-cafe-shrine-hall.jpg is a Google Maps contributor photo (Mankiw Lee,
Sep 2025) of the shrine hall at 喜馬拉雅珈琲 Himalaya Cafe, 新北市林口區民族路81號1樓 —
the Day 1 family visit. The Google Maps interface has been cropped out of it.

It is a reference picture, not a licensed one. Keep this site private rather
than publishing it, and swap in your own photograph when you have one.

To add your own — a family photo from the visit, for example — drop the file in
this folder and add its filename to the `images` array of the
"Himalaya Cafe (喜馬拉雅珈琲), Linkou" entry in assets/data/day1.js:

    images: [
      "himalaya-cafe/himalaya-cafe-family-photo.jpg",
      "himalaya-cafe/himalaya-cafe-shrine-hall.jpg"
    ]

A place card shows up to four photos; whichever is listed first leads.
